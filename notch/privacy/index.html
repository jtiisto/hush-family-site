Privacy policy — Notch

Effective 2026-09-15. Applies to the Android app "Notch: Tinnitus Sound Relief" (package dev.jtiisto.notch).

The short version

Notch collects no data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs that phone home, and no network access at all: the app does not declare the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings — the pitch you matched, the notch width and on/off state, the chosen sound, the volume, the sleep-timer preferences and the matching-tone preferences — are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are included in Android's standard app backup (Google's cloud backup or a device-to-device transfer) if you have that enabled; that backup is governed by Google's terms, not by Notch.
  • Crash reports. If Notch crashes, it writes a plain-text report (time, app version, device model, Android version and the error trace) to its private storage on your device. It never leaves the device unless *you* tap "Share report", which opens Android's share sheet so you choose where it goes. You can delete it from the same screen.

Permissions and why

  • Foreground service / media playback — keeps sound playing while the screen is off and shows the playback notification.
  • Notifications — the playback controls in the notification shade.
  • Wake lock — keeps the audio thread running during deep sleep.

No permission gives Notch access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, files or identity.

Children

Notch is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new effective date. The app itself does not update silently: a new build is required to change what it does.

Contact
